Privacy Policy

How Planovar collects, uses and protects personal information when you use our platform.

Effective date · October 16, 2026 Last updated · October 16, 2026

Effective Date: October 16, 2026

Last Updated: October 16, 2026

Planovar Inc. ("Planovar," "Company," "we," "us," or "our") operates the Planovar mobile application and the website located at planovar.com (collectively, the "Platform"). Planovar provides a global marketplace connecting event hosts and clients ("Customers") with third-party event professionals and service providers ("Vendors").

This Privacy Policy sets out the principles and practices governing our collection, use, disclosure, and protection of personal data gathered from individuals who access or use our Platform worldwide. By downloading, registering with, or using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to operate the Platform, fulfill our contractual obligations, verify platform participants, and maintain the safety and integrity of our global marketplace.

A. Information You Provide Directly

Account and Profile Information: When you register an account as a Customer or Vendor, we collect personal details including your full name, business name, email address, phone number, physical address, profile photographs, and general location details (city and country).

  • Vendor KYC and Identity Verification Data: To complete our Know Your Customer (KYC) process, Vendors must submit official government-issued identification documents (such as a passport or driver's license), business registration certificates, tax identification numbers, and utility bills or proof of address.
  • Communications: We collect and store the content of messages, inquiries, ratings, and reviews exchanged between Customers and Vendors using our in-app messaging features.
  • Customer Support Inquiries: When you contact us for assistance, we store the correspondence, contact details, and any supporting files you submit.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device and Technical Identifiers: When you access the Platform, our servers automatically record your internet protocol (IP) address, mobile device identifiers (such as IMEI or advertising ID), operating system version, browser type, hardware model, and system configuration.
  • Usage Data: We collect logs detailing your interactions with the Platform, including search queries, profiles viewed, pages visited, time spent on specific features, app launch timestamps, and error reports.
  • Location Data: With your explicit permission, we collect precise or approximate geolocation data from your mobile device to show nearby Vendors, filter service availability, and display localized content.

C. Payment Information Disclaimer

Planovar processes vendor subscription billing through secure third-party payment gateways. Planovar does not directly process, store, or handle financial transactions or bank transfers for event services booked between Customers and Vendors.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process personal data based on legitimate business interests, contractual necessity, legal obligations, and user consent:

  • Platform Operations: To create and manage user accounts, display Vendor listings, enable search and discovery, and facilitate direct communications between Customers and Vendors.
  • Identity Verification: To perform background, identity, and business checks via KYC procedures for Vendors seeking verified status.
  • Subscription Management: To process recurring monthly subscription fees for Vendors across Basic, Premium, and Gold subscription tiers.
  • Security and Fraud Prevention: To detect, investigate, and prevent unauthorized account access, fraudulent listings, policy violations, and security incidents.
  • Service Improvements: To analyze user trends, evaluate platform functionality, fix technical bugs, and optimize app performance.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable statutory obligations, tax regulations, court orders, and lawful government requests.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Planovar does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. We share data only under the following conditions:

  • Public Listings and Customer Outreach: Vendor profile details, business names, service portfolios, pricing guidelines, geographic coverage, ratings, and reviews are visible publicly on the Platform.
  • Third-Party Service Providers: We share personal data with vetted service providers who perform operations on our behalf, including identity verification vendors, cloud hosting providers, customer support software, and analytics providers. These providers are bound by strict confidentiality and data processing agreements.
  • Subscription Payment Gateway: We share billing information with authorized payment processing partners solely to charge and maintain Vendor subscription tiers.
  • Corporate Restructuring: In the event of a merger, acquisition, corporate reorganization, asset sale, or bankruptcy, user data may be transferred as part of the relevant business assets.
  • Legal Necessity: We reserve the right to disclose personal data if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to enforce our legal rights and protect the safety of our users.

4. International Data Transfers

Planovar Inc. is based in the United States and operates a global platform. Information collected through the Platform may be transferred to, stored in, and processed within the United States or other international jurisdictions where our service providers operate. Data protection laws in these countries may differ from those in your jurisdiction. We implement appropriate safeguards, including standard contractual clauses, to ensure personal data transferred internationally remains protected in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide you with access to the Platform. We also retain and use personal data to comply with statutory legal requirements, resolve disputes, maintain accurate financial records, and enforce our contractual agreements. When personal data is no longer required, we securely delete or anonymize it.

6. Data Rights and Choices

Depending on your place of residence, you may possess specific statutory rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: The right to request confirmation of whether we process your data and to obtain a copy of that data.
  • Correction: The right to request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Deletion: The right to request the erasure of your personal data, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Restriction and Objection: The right to restrict or object to our processing of your personal data under certain circumstances.
  • Data Portability: The right to receive a copy of your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, please submit a request to info@planovar.com. We may verify your identity before responding to your request.

7. Security Measures

Planovar maintains administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal data against unauthorized access, destruction, loss, alteration, or disclosure. However, no electronic transmission over the internet or method of digital storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Third-Party Links

The Platform may contain links to external websites or third-party services not owned or controlled by Planovar. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party platforms. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party site you visit.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our legal obligations or operational practices. When material updates occur, we will post the revised policy on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date and notify registered users through the Platform or via email.
